Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, during the beta. If we introduce paid plans we'll give account owners at least 30 days' notice, and nobody is charged without choosing a plan.

Does Company Compass submit my returns to SARS?

No. It prepares the figures and files you need (VAT201 figures, EMP201 amounts, IRP5s and the e@syFile export), and you or your accountant submit them on eFiling or e@syFile. Always check figures before you submit.

Do I still need an accountant?

Company Compass keeps your day-to-day books in order; many businesses still use an accountant for year-end and tax. Invite them in with their own login so they can work from the same numbers.

Can I bring my existing data across?

You can import bank statements, revenue and expenses from CSV files, and enter opening balances for your bank accounts, assets and loans.

Where is my data stored?

In a secure database hosted in Ireland (EU), with private file storage for your documents. The Privacy Policy lists every service provider involved.

Can my staff use it?

Yes. Add as many users as you need and choose which parts of the app each person can see, from invoicing only to full access.
